Pre-token protocols, ranked by implied value

The watchlist is the floor: every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record. The radar adds every other pre-token protocol below that floor, prices each one from the market's own multiples, and shows how to farm it. Implied value is a model, not a promise, and no token here is guaranteed to launch.

How to read this radar

Coverage first: every protocol on the snapshot's 40-protocol watchlist is ranked here, plus every other pre-token protocol that passes the watchlist's own test: no wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, no exchange product, no versioned pool. The price: each row's multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in its own category, so a category the market prices richly prices its pre-token names richly too; growth and age then move it. The plan: every row carries six farm steps with a cost and a time, derived from its category, chain, size and age. Live-token protocols are set aside in their own tab with their market caps shown, never mixed into the ranking.

Data: a sealed DefiLlama snapshot. "Records no token" means DefiLlama records no token for the protocol; it is not proof none exists. Nothing here is financial advice.
